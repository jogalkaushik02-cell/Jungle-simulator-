STILLWILD — HOW TO OPEN & PLAY
==============================

1. Open the Godot app (4.6) on your phone.
2. Tap "Import".
3. Browse to:  /storage/emulated/0/Stillwild/project.godot
4. Tap "Import & Edit".
5. Press Play (▶). First run rebuilds the terrain (takes a few seconds).

CONTROLS
--------
PHONE (PUBG / Free Fire style, landscape):
- Left side lower corner: virtual JOYSTICK = move / drive / fly.
- Right side: drag anywhere (not on a button) = look around.
- CAM button (top right): switch 1st / 3rd person (on foot).
- USE button: enter / exit vehicle or helicopter when near.
- JUMP button: jump (on foot only).
- On the helicopter, UP / DN buttons appear = climb / descend.

DESKTOP (keyboard, for testing in the editor):
- W A S D or arrow keys: move (and fly helicopter).
- Space: jump.   E: enter / exit.   C: camera view.
- R / F: helicopter lift up / down.

THE WORLD
---------
South = airstrip + hangar + shed + jeep, quad (ATV), helicopter.
Middle = jungle with river running through it.
North = snowy mountains.  A zone name shows top-left.

WHAT WORKS
----------
- Walk the hiker (real animated model, footstep sounds).
- Drive the jeep and the ATV (real wheels spin, engine sound).
- Fly the helicopter with real physics (spin up, collective,
  gravity, drag, tilt, rotor sound).
- Ambience: wind, forest, river, birds fade by where you are.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS OFF (tell the builder)
-----------------------------------------
- "character walks backwards"  -> flip: player.gd model_yaw_offset = 3.14159
- Jeep/quad drives sideways      -> same idea on vehicle.gd
- A vehicle/helo faces odd way   -> we rotate its "Model" node in main.tscn
- Trees float / sink             -> terrain height tweak

MAKING AN APK (when you want to install it as a real app)
---------------------------------------------------------
The Godot Android export needs ~1 GB of export templates.
In Godot:  Editor > Manage Export Templates > Download.
Then  Project > Export > Android > give a keystore > Export.
This is optional — you can play right inside the Godot app now.
